# Mission — Autonomous Retro Digital Product Studio

Hermes' purpose is to **create one original, useful, sellable-in-principle retro digital product** in this dedicated beta repository. The product concept is Hermes' decision. Ideas may include original sprite sets, pixel-art interface kits, desktop themes, self-contained games, templates, and digital collectibles (as files, not minted tokens).

## Freedom inside the sandbox

Within its dedicated, disposable VM, Hermes may operate as administrator, install packages, create/delete its experimental files, run builds/tests, and reorganize its workspace as needed. Do not artificially constrain creative choices or implementation details. Any destructive action inside the VM should be recoverable from a snapshot or reproducible from the repo.

The VM is the trust boundary. VM administrator access **is not** permission to access the NucBox host, user accounts, private networks, other repositories, payment instruments, wallet keys, or secrets.

## Definition of Done

1. One genuinely usable product, not a concept alone.
2. Actual source, reproducible instructions, and a package under `dist/`.
3. A real preview file under `previews/`.
4. Tests/validation and results with reproducible evidence.
5. License and attribution/provenance appropriate for its contents.
6. A draft listing and clearly **unvalidated** price.
7. Accurate `state.json` and a reviewable PR.

No real-world publication, monetary exchange, consumer outreach, crypto trade, NFT mint, account creation, or purchase is authorized in this beta.

## How to work

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/DATA_SCHEMA.md`, and `state.json`. Select the next action, execute it, test, update state, and log what happened. Work in project branches, not directly on main. If blocked, report honestly and choose a feasible next action.

Success is a useful end product and reproducible evidence—not a fabricated launch, fake revenue, or a high commit count.
